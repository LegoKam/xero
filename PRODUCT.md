# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small business owners, accountants and bookkeepers in Australia (and other Xero regions) evaluating or using Xero cloud accounting. _provenance: inferred from nav ("Small Business", "Accountants & Bookkeepers") and the /au page copy._

## Product Purpose

Xero is online accounting and business software. This site sells the product: plans and pricing, features, and routes to a free trial ("Get one month free"). _provenance: inferred from the header, hero ("Small business accounting to set you free") and the plans section of /au._

## Brand Personality

Friendly, plain-spoken, confident, benefit-led. _provenance: inferred from captured headlines and CTA labels._

## Anti-references

Dense enterprise-software chrome, stock-photo clichés, and jargon-heavy copy.

## Design Principles

1. Navy text on white with generous space; mint green reserved for the primary action.
2. Rounded, approachable shapes (6px buttons, 12px panels).
3. Lead with the benefit, then the proof (awards, subscriber counts).
